A useful clue, with clear limits

Photo suggestions with uncertainty visible.

Experimental photo comparison runs on your device using bundled reference specimens for calcite, chalcopyrite, fluorite, granite and marble. It may suggest several visual matches, be wrong, or leave a find unidentified.

Visual matches are not laboratory identification, valuation or confirmation that a specimen is safe. Ask a qualified professional when a find may be hazardous or important.

Offline, without an account

Your collection stays with you.

Your saved collection and photo comparison work offline, without an account or an external API. Stone Keeper includes no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.
